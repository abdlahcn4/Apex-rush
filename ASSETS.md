# Apex Rush — imported asset register

Every file listed here was **downloaded into this repository** and is imported by Godot at
build time. Nothing in this list is "planned"; if a file is in `godot/assets/`, it is real
and it ships in the APK.

## Where the assets came from (and what could not be reached)

The sandbox this project is built in has outbound network access to **GitHub, npm and PyPI
only**. Sketchfab, Kenney.nl, Poly Haven, ambientCG, OpenGameArt and Freesound are all
blocked, so nothing could be fetched from those sites directly. Everything below therefore
comes from **GitHub-hosted mirrors of CC0 packs**, and every licence was checked before use.

| Asset set | Upstream author | Licence | Mirror used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Car Kit (14 vehicles + 4 wheels + colormap) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | `github.com/shorepine/kenney` (`3d/car`) |
| Racing Kit (32 road pieces, 44 track props, 4 race cars) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | `github.com/rakesh-ranj/lane-dash` (`assets/kenney_racing_kit`) |
| Sci-fi engine loops (5 `.ogg`) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | `github.com/OmniTender-Systems/game-audio-assets` |
| Impact / UI sound effects (`.wav`) | Wyzowl SFX Kit | CC0 1.0 | `github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit` |
| Asphalt / concrete / grass / gravel PBR sets (albedo + normal + roughness) | ambientCG | CC0 1.0 | `github.com/petroulacl/fps-buildings-env-kit` (`environment/ground-textures/ambientcg`, 2K JPG sets) |

CC0 1.0 requires no attribution; it is recorded here anyway so the provenance is auditable.

### Known limitations, stated plainly

- The ambientCG textures are the **real 2048 px PBR sets** (Asphalt021, Concrete012,
  Grass004, Ground037, each with Colour, NormalGL and Roughness), downscaled to 1024 and
  re-encoded as JPEG for the mobile build. They are scans, not previews.
- The first build shipped ambientCG **catalogue preview sheets** by mistake: single images
  containing 40 labelled tiles. Tiled as a surface they produced the white grid and the
  mirrored "Asphalt" lettering that was visible on the road. Those files have been deleted
  and are replaced by the per-material sets above.
- No HDRI skybox could be obtained: every GitHub-hosted `.hdr` found (Blender demo
  environments) is a **Git LFS pointer**, and LFS media is not reachable from this sandbox.
  The sky is therefore Godot's built-in `ProceduralSkyMaterial` (engine atmosphere shader,
  not hand-built geometry), driven by real sun/physical scattering parameters.
- Kenney's kits are **stylised low-poly**, professionally modelled and textured but not
  photoreal. They are genuine imported art (modelled bodywork, rims, spoilers, grandstands,
  pits, billboards, trees), not primitives generated by script.

## What is generated in code, and why

The project keeps its physics, track spline and camera systems from the original port.
Those generate **invisible or topological data** (sample points, containment, wall limits,
waypoints), not objects the player looks at. The exceptions that remain visible are listed
below so nothing is oversold:

- the ground plane and the far mountain silhouette (backdrop, outside the playable area);
- the road *ribbon* mesh, which follows the Catmull-Rom spline the vehicle physics is tuned
  against, surfaced with the imported ambientCG asphalt texture;
- the sky (engine shader, see above).

Everything the player drives past — cars, wheels, barriers, grandstands, billboards, light
posts, pit buildings, trees — is an imported glTF model.

### Stated limitations (this round)

- No isolated CC0 **turbo whistle / blow-off / exhaust-pop** recordings were reachable: every
  repository hosting them declares no licence. The three are therefore Kenney CC0
  recordings re-pitched and gated, not dedicated samples. This is the honest description.
- HDRIs ship at 1k (1024×512) to keep the APK inside the size budget; the night map uses a
  separate HDRI rather than a day/night shader conversion.
- There is one authored car model (CarConcept). All six tiers drive it, differentiated by
  paint, ride height and the length/wheelbase from `CarBuilder.SPECS`, so the grid is
  visually coherent rather than six recolours of a Kenney cube car. The old procedural
  builder is retained only as a fallback if the GLB is missing, and is never used when the
  imported model is present.
- The cockpit camera anchors to the model's `InteriorSteeringWheel01` node, so it sits at
  the driver's eye point on every car.
